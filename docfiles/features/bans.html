Features

Ban, Temp Ban and Unban

Bans block a player from joining. They can be permanent or timed, can include the player's IP address, and work for players who are offline or have never joined.

Commands​

CommandDescriptionPermission
/ban <player> [duration] [reason]Permanent ban, or timed if a duration is givenserveradmincore.ban
/tempban <player> <duration> [reason]Timed banserveradmincore.tempban
/ipban <player> [duration] [reason]Ban the player and their last known IPserveradmincore.ban.ip
/unban <player|id>Remove a banserveradmincore.unban
/bans [count]List recent bansserveradmincore.ban
/ban Bob Cheating
/ban Bob 7d Cheating
/tempban Bob 12h Griefing
/ipban Bob Ban evasion
/ban id:76561198000000000 Known cheater
/unban Bob
tip

serveradmincore.tempban lets moderators ban for a limited time without being able to hand out permanent bans. A moderator with only tempban also can't replace an existing permanent ban.

Offline players​

Players who have joined before can be banned by name or id. Players who have never joined can be banned with id:<PlayerId>, so you can pre-ban known cheaters from other servers.

What the player sees​

When banned while online, the player is kicked with the Ban Message or Temp Ban Message. When they try to rejoin, login is rejected with the same message. Both support {Reason}, {Duration}, {Expires} and {By}.

Expiry​

Timed bans are removed automatically when they expire, and the removal is logged as an Unban by Expired.

C++​

// Anti-cheat: ban for 30 days, including IP.
ServerAdminCore->BanPlayer(FServerAdminCoreCaller::System(TEXT("AntiCheat")), PlayerId,
    TEXT("Speed hack detected"), 30 * 24 * 3600, /*bBanIPAddress*/ true);

Player Notes

Notes are private messages from staff to staff about a player: "Warned for spawn camping", "Suspected aimbot, watch". Players never see them.

Commands​

CommandDescriptionPermission
/note <player> <text>Add a noteserveradmincore.notes.add
/notes <player>Read notes, newest firstserveradmincore.notes.view
/delnote <note id>Delete a noteserveradmincore.notes.remove
/note Bob Warned for team killing
/notes Bob
  [3f2a9c1d] 2026-10-12 19:04 by Alice: Warned for team killing
/delnote 3f2a9c1d

The first 8 characters of a note id are enough to delete it.

Notes work for offline players and for players who never joined (id:<PlayerId>).
